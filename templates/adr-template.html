Status

[Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded by ADR-NNN]

Update this line whenever the decision's state changes. If superseded, link to the ADR that replaces it.

Context

What situation or problem forced this decision? Include the forces at play — technical constraints, team agreements, external requirements, time pressure — so future readers understand why this choice was made at this point in time rather than a different one.

Decision

What was decided, stated in full sentences. Be specific enough that someone reading this in a year can tell exactly what was implemented, configured, or rejected.

Consequences

What becomes easier? What becomes harder? Any trade-offs accepted?

List the effects — positive and negative — so the full cost of the decision is on record. Include any follow-up work this decision creates.
